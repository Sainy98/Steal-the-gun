A crash course.

01

Stay on the platforms.

Hits increase your knockback meter. The higher it gets, the farther you fly. You have a double jump. Falling out eliminates you until the next short round.

02

Bad weapon? Bump someone.

Dash into a rival to exchange weapons—including remaining ammo. Dash recharges in 1.8 seconds. After a swap, both players get a short swap lock. Glowing spawn shields temporarily prevent hits and swaps.

03

Physics is your frenemy.

Cannon recoil pushes you backwards. Bowling balls bounce. Bubbles lift. Balloons can be popped by projectiles. Spring punches knock rivals away. The vacuum pulls them closer.

04

Eight rounds. One bragging right.

Last survivor earns a round win. Platforms begin collapsing after 35 seconds. At 60 seconds, surviving players with the lowest knockback meter share the win. Final ties use knockouts, then fewer falls; exact ties share victory.
